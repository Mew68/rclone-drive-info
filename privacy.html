Privacy Policy

This application is used by its owner to access their own Google Drive files through rclone.

Google Drive data is accessed only to provide file browsing, uploading, downloading, and other file operations requested by the owner.

The application does not operate a separate server or sell Google user data. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally by rclone on the owner's computer. The owner is responsible for protecting that computer and its configuration.

No Google Drive data is intentionally shared with third parties by this project, apart from requests necessary to Google's services and the owner's chosen storage tools.
